Policies & FAQ

🚚 Delivery Areas & Pickup

We currently offer hand-delivery within Patna, Bihar. Delivery charges apply based on your exact pincode. You are also welcome to opt for "Store Pickup" directly from our studio in Krishi Nagar at no extra charge.

⏱️ 24-Hour Lead Time

To guarantee the freshest possible product, we do not keep baked cakes in stock. Every order is made from scratch upon payment confirmation. We strictly require a 24-hour notice for all standard menu items, and 48-72 hours for highly customized theme cakes.

❌ Cancellations & Refunds

Because our products are perishable and made to order, cancellations must be made at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled delivery/pickup time for a full refund. Cancellations made within 24 hours cannot be refunded.

Cake Care Instructions

  • ❄️ Storage: Store cream cakes in the refrigerator immediately upon arrival. Do not freeze.
  • 🌡️ Serving: For the best taste and softest sponge, remove the cake from the fridge 30-45 minutes before cutting.
  • 🚗 Transporting: Place the box flat on the floorboard of your car with the AC on. Do not place on car seats.
  • 🗓️ Shelf Life: Best consumed within 2-3 days for maximum freshness.
